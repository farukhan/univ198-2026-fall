Group Study Room Policy
Campus Learning Commons (an invented facility for UNIV198)
Version 2. Effective 1 September 2025. Superseded by version 3 on 1 September 2026.

1. Purpose and scope
  1.1 This policy applies to the seven group study rooms on floors 1 and 2 of the Learning Commons.
  1.2 It replaces the 2024 policy (version 1).

2. Who may book, and how much
  2.1 Any registered student may book a room for a group of at least three members. A group may book up to 8 hours per week, in blocks of no more than 3 hours.
  2.2 Bookings open 14 days ahead and are made online through the Learning Commons booking page.

3. Arriving, cancelling and no-shows
  3.1 A booking may be cancelled online up to 2 hours before its start.
  3.2 If no member of the group has arrived 10 minutes after the start of the booking, the booking is cancelled and the room is released to the next group.
  3.3 A cancellation later than 2 hours before the start, or a non-arrival under 3.2, counts as a no-show. Three no-shows in a semester suspend the group's booking rights for the rest of the semester.

4. Using the room
  4.1 Food and drink are not allowed in the rooms.
  4.2 Whiteboard markers are available at the service desk against a student ID card, and must be returned at the end of the booking.
  4.3 Leave the room as you found it. Report any damage at the service desk before you leave.
