Lecture 14 in One Page

Week 8 in one sentence. An agent is a model in a loop with tools; a workflow is that loop with a contract around it (trigger, inputs, steps, checkpoints, permissions, done, failure); it goes wrong in five known ways, the defences are design choices made before the first run, and the person who delegates the work keeps the decision and the log.

The seven fields

Trigger · Inputs · Steps and tools · Checkpoints and hand-offs · Permissions · Done means · If a step fails

Permissions and done means first; a stranger could run it from the card alone.

The five failures

Compounding · Prompt injection · Wrong tool, over-reach · Silent failure · Loops and cost

Least permissions; a person at every exit; data stays data; the log, read; caps and minimums.

Three numbers, not a demo

How often it succeeds on ten real cases; what a run costs in minutes, steps and money; how long the check takes. If the check is as long as the task, the agent saved nothing.

When not

Can a rule do it exactly: automate. Same steps every time: a workflow. A step that is irreversible and uncheckable: a person does it. Check longer than the task: do it yourself. Otherwise: an agent, with its card.
