What the Room Usually Finds, and What Comes Next

Permissions rewrite the steps

Once “send” is unticked, the last step becomes a hand-off on nearly every card. The field that took thirty seconds changed the design most.

“If a step fails” is left blank

Half the cards have “retry” and nothing after it. Retry forever is a loop; retry twice then stop and tell someone is a design.

The silent failure is a read

A search that returns one result, a file that opens empty, a page that is a login wall. The agent carries on; the card must say “stop”.

The lesson: a workflow is designed from its edges inwards: what it may never do, what ends it, what stops it. The steps in the middle are the easy part, and the only part most people write.
