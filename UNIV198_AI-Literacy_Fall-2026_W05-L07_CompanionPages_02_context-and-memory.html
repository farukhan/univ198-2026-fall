What the Window Holds in a Long Chat

Everything inside the window is the text the next token is predicted from: the app's instructions, then every turn so far. Press Add a turn and watch what happens to the rule you set in turn 1.

It is large now

In 2026 the chat windows of Microsoft Copilot and Google Gemini hold hundreds of pages. Running out is rare in coursework.

It is weighted unevenly

Text in the middle of a long context is used less reliably than text at the start or the end (Liu et al., 2023, “lost in the middle”). Your turn-1 rule is in the middle by turn 7.

The app edits it

When a chat gets very long, the app may summarise or drop early turns. You are not told which.

Liu, N. F. et al. (2023). Lost in the Middle: How Language Models Use Long Contexts. Transactions of the ACL, 12 (2024), 157–173. arxiv.org/abs/2307.03172

Three Ways a Chat Drifts

Rule fade

A constraint set in turn 1 is obeyed in turn 2 and ignored by turn 8. Nothing was forgotten; it is simply far away and outweighed by recent text.

Topic bleed

An example from an earlier task leaks into a new one: the summary of article A borrows a phrase from article B. Both are in the window.

Anchoring

Its own earlier wrong answer is now text in the window, and text in the window is what it predicts from. Errors compound unless you remove them.

Mechanism, in one sentence: there is no “remembering” and “forgetting”. Every earlier token is in the distribution for the next one, weighted by position and relevance. Drift is weighting, and so are the fixes.

Keeping a chat on track

Restate the rule where it matters

As before: Turkish, two sentences.

Five words in the turn that needs them beat a paragraph nine turns back.

Pin the format

Same table as your last answer, same columns.

Names the shape explicitly instead of hoping it persists.

Number your requests

Request 3 of 4: ...

Lets you refer back precisely (“redo request 2”) and stops the model merging tasks.

New task, new chat

[new chat] [the brief] [the task]

Unrelated task, or a drifted chat: start again. Carry over only a brief. Topic bleed cannot cross chats.

The Brief: Three Lines That Restart a Chat

Write yours for one chat you will actually open this week (a course, a project, a club). Fill the three lines; the brief assembles below. Copy it to the top of the new chat, and keep it in your notes: it is also your disclosure record.

1. Behaviour and reader. Not a job title: what the answer should do, and for whom.

2. The task, and what is already decided. The format, the length, the decisions from the last chat you want kept.

3. Rules and the check. One negative rule with its positive twin, and the verification step.

Your brief

Test: paste the brief, run one task. Is the first answer better than your usual first answer? If yes, the brief has earned its place. A good brief is recognisable because you can predict what the first answer will look like before you send it.

“Memory” in the 2026 Chat Apps

Both Microsoft Copilot and Google Gemini now offer memory or personalisation: the app stores facts about you (your field, your preferences, things you said) and inserts them into the window of later chats.

What it is

Text added to the context by the app. The model has not learned anything; the window is longer. Lecture 3's picture still holds.

What to do

Open the memory page in the app settings. Read what it holds. Delete what is wrong or stale. For coursework, know what is there or turn it off.

What never goes in

Other people's data (Lecture 4). A stored fact about a classmate, patient or client is a leak you cannot see on screen.

Why it matters today: a stored preference is a turn-0 rule you did not write this time. If an answer is oddly shaped, check memory before you blame your prompt.

Takeaways

The window is weighted, not remembered

Early rules fade because they are far away. Restate them where they matter.

New task, new chat

Carry over a brief, not the history. Topic bleed and anchoring cannot cross chats.

The brief is three lines

Behaviour and reader; task and what is decided; rules and the check. Paste it at the top of every new chat on that task.

Memory is text you did not write this time

Read it, prune it, and never let other people's data into it.
